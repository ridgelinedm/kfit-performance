FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my first session really free?
Yes. Your intro session is completely free and no-pressure. It's a chance to meet your coach, tour the private studio, talk through your goals, and see if KFit is the right fit before you commit to anything.
Do I need experience to start?
Not at all. Many of our members start as complete beginners or are returning to fitness after years away. Every program begins with form coaching and meets you exactly where you are. There's no judgment here — just coaching.
What's the difference between the plans?
The plans differ mainly in how often you train each week: Foundations is 2 sessions, Momentum is 3, and Elite is 5 with more frequent check-ins and full customization. All three include private gym access, progress tracking, mobility work, nutrition ideas, and accountability.
Are sessions one-on-one?
Yes — your training is personalized and coached. You'll work directly with a trainer who programs around your body, your goals, and your schedule, and adjusts as you progress.
Can I switch plans later?
Absolutely. As your goals and schedule change, we'll help you move up or down between Foundations, Momentum, and Elite so your membership always fits your life.
Do you offer nutrition coaching?
Every plan includes practical nutrition ideas and accountability — simple, sustainable guidance rather than restrictive crash diets. We focus on habits you can actually keep.
Where are you located?
We're at 2830 Wade Hampton Blvd, Suite 35, Taylors, SC 29687 — convenient to Greenville, Greer, Mauldin, Simpsonville, and Travelers Rest. See our contact page for a map and hours.
How do I get started?
Just book your free session. We'll reach out to schedule a time that works, and you'll be training before you know it.
