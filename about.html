About KFit

More than a gym.
A place that moves with you.

KFit Performance is a private personal training studio in Taylors, SC — built for real people who want coaching that actually fits their life.

Our Story

Built for the people gyms forget

KFit Performance started with a frustration shared by so many: walking into a big-box gym and feeling completely on your own. Crowded floors, no real guidance, programs copied from a stranger's feed — it's a recipe for quitting.

So we built something different. A private studio where the room is calm, the coaching is personal, and the only person you're competing with is who you were last week. Whether you're brand new, getting back after years away, or chasing a serious goal, you'll never be just another membership here.

Our name says it: Set the Pace. Your pace, your goals, your life — we move with you.

Studio interior — turf, racks, open training floor
Our Mission

Personalized training. Real results. A community that moves with you.

We exist to make great coaching accessible to everyday people — beginners, busy adults, older adults, and anyone who's tried and failed at fitness before. Direct, honest, and warm. No hype, no judgment.

People First

You're a person, not a punch card. We learn your story, your limits, and your goals — then coach accordingly.

Results That Last

Sustainable progress beats quick fixes. We build strength and habits that hold up long after the session ends.

Community That Moves

Beneath the performance edge is a genuine community — people who notice when you show up and cheer when you win.

Private gym — equipment detail / coaching in action
The Studio

A private gym, built for focus

KFit Performance is a members-and-clients-only studio — not an open-to-the-public floor. That means no waiting for equipment, no crowds, and no self-conscious workouts. Just a clean, well-equipped space designed for serious training without the intimidation.

Every session is coached. You'll have access to the tools you need for strength, conditioning, mobility, and progress tracking — all under the eye of a trainer who knows exactly where you are in your journey.

Meet the Trainers

The coaches in your corner

A small, dedicated team that knows your name, your goals, and your progress.

Kylie Pace headshot — coaching portrait

Kylie Pace

Owner & Head Coach

Kylie founded KFit Performance to give the Upstate the kind of coaching she always wished existed — personal, honest, and genuinely encouraging. She specializes in meeting clients exactly where they are and building a plan they can actually stick to. When she's not coaching, she's the heartbeat of the KFit community.

Katherine Watson headshot — coaching portrait

Katherine Watson

Personal Trainer

Katherine has a gift for making beginners feel at home under the bar. Patient and detail-focused, she coaches technique first so clients build strength safely and confidently. Busy adults love how she keeps training efficient without ever cutting corners.

Todd Reeves headshot — coaching portrait

Todd Reeves

Personal Trainer

Todd brings a strength-and-conditioning background and an easy, motivating coaching style. He's worked with clients of every age and ability, and he's especially skilled at helping people return to training after injury or a long time away.
